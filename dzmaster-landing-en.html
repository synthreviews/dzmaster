No parameters, no tweaking. Pick the platform you're releasing to and the tool handles the rest.

Frequently asked questions

Is DZMASTER free?
The Smart Auto preset is, and stays, free, with no sign-up and no limit. The other nine presets (Streaming, Apple/Podcast, Club/EDM and six more) unlock with a one-time code you get on Gumroad, and once unlocked, mastering has no limit of any kind.
Is my track uploaded to a server?
No. The entire mastering chain runs locally in your browser. Your file is never uploaded anywhere and leaves no trace once you close the page.
What file format does DZMASTER support?
Input is WAV (16, 24 or 32-bit, mono or stereo). Output is a mastered WAV at 16 or 24-bit quality.
Does DZMASTER replace a professional mastering engineer?
Not entirely — it performs basic but technically correct mastering (loudness, safe peaks, gentle dynamics evening) without creative EQ shaping of your mix. For a major release a human mastering engineer is still the better choice; for demos, singles or podcasts, DZMASTER is a fast, reliable solution.
How long does processing take?
A typical track (3-5 minutes) processes in a few seconds to under a minute, depending on your device's performance.
